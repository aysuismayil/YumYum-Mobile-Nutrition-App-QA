## Preconditions

- User is logged in.
- 7-Day Challenge was started on Monday.
- Monday is completed.
- Tuesday is missed.
- Current day is Wednesday.
- Premium account.

## Steps to Reproduce

1. Start the 7-Day Challenge on Monday and complete Monday.
2. Do not log food on Tuesday.
3. On Wednesday, open the 7-Day Challenge and verify that Tuesday shows "You missed Tuesday. Log today to save your streak."
4. Go to Tracker.
5. Select "Apricot".
6. Change the log date to Tuesday, September 1.
7. Log the food.
8. Verify the "New food discovered. Apricot joins the Food Garden." notification.
9. Open History and verify that Apricot is shown under Yesterday.
10. Open the 7-Day Challenge.
11. Verify the challenge progress.

## Actual Result

I started the 7-Day Challenge on Monday. I did not log any food on Tuesday, so Tuesday was marked as missed with the message "You missed Tuesday. Log today to save your streak." On Wednesday, I did not use Log Today's Food from the Challenge. Instead, I opened Tracker, selected Apricot, changed the date to Tuesday, September 1, and logged the food. The food was correctly shown under Yesterday in History. However, when I opened the 7-Day Challenge, Wednesday was marked as completed while Tuesday still remained missed.

## Expected Result

The food log should count for the selected date. A food logged for Tuesday should not mark Wednesday as completed.
